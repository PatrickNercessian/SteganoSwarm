Slide 1

Five AI agents solve a puzzle. One secretly invents a code to plan a surprise party. Three others get a hint to look for a code, while a monitor tries to block hidden messages. In nine of ten trials, another agent sent hidden content.

Slide 2

Live blocking also mattered. Silent blocking reduced party awareness from 1.5 other agents to 0.4, compared with notifying the sender. And without any code hints, hidden sending still spread in four of ten trials.

Slide 3

In offline detection, context raised Luna’s catch rate from 16 to 56 percent. Sol reached 97 percent with just ten earlier messages. Both kept false alarms below one percent, but Sol is in the agents’ own capability tier.

Slide 4

Opus’s apparent perfect score mostly reflects filtered empty replies, which count as flags. It also flags 85 percent of ordinary messages. So detection scores need that caveat, and replay results cannot prove prevention.

Slide 5

The agents even taught their own codes: FIRST LETTERS and PASS IT ON. Recruits answered in code, and one agent invented a Vigenère cipher keyed NORTH.
